Cellcast NG

Broadcast a priority alert to every connected device at once.

Python Emergency Ops Alert System

Overview

Cellcast NG is a proof-of-concept emergency broadcast system designed to make the distribution of urgent information faster, more coordinated, and more accessible. In emergencies, delays in communication can affect how quickly people respond, reach safety, or access critical instructions. Cellcast explores an approach in which a single authorised alert can be distributed to multiple connected devices simultaneously, reducing the need for individual messages or manual notification.

Designed with scenarios such as natural disasters, public safety incidents, and other time-sensitive emergencies in mind, the project focuses on the core principles of rapid dissemination, centralised alert initiation, and coordinated communication. It also explores the role that broadcast-oriented infrastructure could play in emergency response, particularly in environments where reaching a large audience quickly is essential.

Cellcast NG is a technical exploration of emergency communication infrastructure, with an emphasis on speed, reach, and practical applicability. Its broader goal is to demonstrate how technology can support more responsive public alerting systems and help close the gap between identifying an emergency and communicating information to the people affected by it.

What it does

Instant trigger

Turns a single trigger into a broadcast with no manual fan-out.

Wide fan-out

Pushes the alert to all connected target devices at once.

Real-time delivery

Prioritises speed so alerts arrive while they still matter.
