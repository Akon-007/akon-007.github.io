Fake Login Detector Extension

Your browser's early warning for credential-stealing login pages.

JavaScript Browser Phishing

Overview

Fake Login Detector Extension is a browser extension engineered to inspect webpage DOM elements, heuristics, and URL signatures in real time to intercept and flag deceptive authentication interfaces.

The tool provides instant heuristic warnings and visual threat indicators to users before credential submission, combining lightweight client-side execution with rapid pattern matching against known phishing vectors.

Designed to safeguard online users from credential theft, the project focuses on proactive browser-level protection, rapid inspection, and intuitive threat visualization.

What it does

Flags suspicious pages

Highlights login pages that show signs of being fake.

Real-time detection

Checks pages as you browse instead of after the fact.

Credential protection

Helps stop passwords being entered into phishing forms.
