Simple Port Scanner

Fast, multi-threaded TCP reconnaissance with banner grabbing built in.

Python Networking Recon

Overview

Simple Port Scanner is a lightweight network reconnaissance utility programmed to probe target hosts for open ports and active TCP/UDP services.

It provides fast socket-level enumeration capabilities to help administrators map network attack surfaces and identify unauthorized listening services.

The project offers a straightforward yet effective exploration of network discovery techniques, emphasizing speed, accuracy, and reconnaissance fundamentals.

What it does

Multi-threaded scanning

Checks many ports at once to keep reconnaissance fast.

Banner grabbing

Reads the greeting a service returns to help identify what's running.

Service detection

Maps open ports to the services behind them, not just open/closed states.

Clear results

Summarises findings in a readable format you can act on.
