About Me

I'm a cybersecurity student with a deep interest in understanding how systems can be compromised — and how to defend them. I enjoy working through CTF challenges, studying malware behavior, and building security tools from scratch.

My goal is to specialize in penetration testing and red-teaming, helping organizations identify and fix vulnerabilities before attackers can exploit them.

When I'm not studying or hacking boxes on HackTheBox, you'll find me contributing to open-source security projects or writing up CTF walkthroughs.
